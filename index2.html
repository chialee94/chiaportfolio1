About Me

From a young age, I have been passionate about creating and designing. My first experience with HTML and CSS came from customizing my Myspace profile, which sparked my curiosity about digital design and visual storytelling. Alongside this, I have pursued photography, developing an eye for composition, aesthetics, and detail.

I enjoy bringing ideas to life through design, both professionally and personally. In my free time, I create small projects in Photoshop, such as banners and flyers, honing my visual and creative skills. These experiences have shaped my approach to design, emphasizing creativity, attention to detail, and user-focused aesthetics.

Looking ahead, I aspire to grow as a UX/UI designer, creating intuitive, visually engaging experiences that combine functionality with design excellence.
